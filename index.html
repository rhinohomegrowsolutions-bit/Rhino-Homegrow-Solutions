What We Do

Practical grow support without the confusion.

Whether you need a full setup plan, help dialing in your tent, or ongoing guidance, Rhino Homegrow Solutions gives you a clear path to follow.

🌱

Setup and Planning

We help plan your grow space, equipment, lighting, airflow, containers, soil, and schedule. You also receive a personalized step-by-step guide to follow for a simple, stress-free grow.

🎥

Remote Video Monitoring

We offer remote video grow monitoring and guidance. With the right setup, we can completely control your grow tent environment, including temperature, humidity, and watering remotely. In that situation, the client simply sources and plants the seed, removes humidity domes when it is time, and adds nutrients at pre-determined times while we help control everything else remotely.

🏔️

Upstate NY Grow Guidance

We specialize in growing in Upstate New York and Adirondack climates, where short outdoor seasons, humidity swings, cool nights, and indoor winter growing all require smart planning.

💡

Beginner-Friendly Education

We explain what matters most in plain language, including lighting, watering, airflow, transplanting, soil health, nutrients, and avoiding common beginner mistakes.

🛠️

Grow Tent Optimization

Already have equipment? We can help improve your layout, airflow, humidity control, light height, watering schedule, and overall environment.

🤝

Ongoing Grow Support

Get friendly grow support throughout the process so you are not guessing every step. We help you stay organized, prepared, and confident as your plants develop.

Built for local growers who want it simple.

Rhino Homegrow Solutions was created to make home growing easier for people in Upstate NY and the Adirondack region. The goal is simple: help clients build a clean, manageable grow system and understand exactly what to do next.

Led by Ryan Brainard, Rhino Homegrow Solutions focuses on practical setup help, clear education, and personalized grow guidance for beginners and everyday home growers.

We help you avoid wasted money, confusing equipment choices, poor airflow, weak lighting, overwatering, and other common issues that slow down a grow before it has a chance to thrive.

Why growers choose Rhino Homegrow Solutions

  • Setup plans designed around your home, budget, and goals.
